What's in Famee

  • A shared calendar

    See everyone's plans by day, week, or month, each person in their own color.

  • Tasks and chores

    Give a job to one person or a few, make it repeat, and check it off when it's done.

  • Lists, recipes, and meals

    Shopping lists everyone can add to, a home for your recipes, and a plan for the week's meals.

  • Ember, when you want help

    Tell Ember what's coming up or send a photo of a recipe, and it fills in the details. Everything also works by hand.
